    Cookie Policy

    Last updated: April 2026

    Whisperbloom Garden Tales (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies to make your visit as pleasant as a stroll through a dew-kissed meadow. This policy explains what cookies we use and how you can manage them.

    What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They help us remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you interact with our content — always anonymously.

    Cookies we use

    • Essential cookies: These are required for the site to function properly (e.g., session management, form submissions).
    • Functional cookies: They remember your choices, such as language preferences or region.
    • Analytics cookies: We use a privacy-friendly, aggregated analytics tool to see which pages are most popular — no personal data is collected.

    We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party that monetizes your data.

    Your consent

    When you first visit our site, we ask for your consent to place non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time via the cookie settings in your browser.

    How to manage cookies

    Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You may choose to block all cookies, but this may affect the functionality of our site.

    Updates

    We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here, and we encourage you to review it from time to time.
