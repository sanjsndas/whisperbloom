    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: April 2026

    Whisperbloom Garden Tales (“we”, “us”, “our”) values the trust you place in us. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our garden—digital or otherwise.

    What we collect

    We limit collection to what is necessary for correspondence and improving your experience:

    • Name and email address — when you send a message or subscribe to our newsletter.
    • Aggregated usage data — such as page visits and search queries, anonymized.
    • Cookies — only essential functional cookies (see our Cookie Policy).

    How we use your data

    • To respond to your inquiries and correspondence.
    • To send occasional garden notes and updates, if you have opted in.
    • To improve our content and understand which topics resonate with our readers.

    We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share data with third parties except as required by law or to protect the security of our site.

    Data security

    We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your data. All transmissions are encrypted, and access is limited to essential personnel.

    Your rights

    You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to privacy@whisperbloom.garden. We will respond within 15 days.

    Updates to this policy

    We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
